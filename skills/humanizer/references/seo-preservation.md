# SEO and Structure Preservation

Apply this in marketing mode (blog posts, landing pages, product pages, marketing emails). The copy usually has to keep ranking and stay eligible for featured snippets and AI answer engines, so humanizing must not quietly break the structure that earns that traffic. Treat these as hard constraints; a user instruction or brand guide can override any of them.

- **Target keyword.** Keep the primary keyword and its key placements: the title/H1, the first ~100 words, and at least one H2. Don't paraphrase it into a synonym. Keep secondary keywords where they already read naturally; don't stuff new ones.
- **Heading hierarchy.** Preserve the H1/H2/H3 levels and their order. You may rewrite heading *text* for tone (including fixing title case per the brand), but don't delete a heading, change its level, or merge a section under its own H2 into a run-on paragraph. This constrains the "merge or split paragraphs freely" latitude in Your Task step 2 (`SKILL.md`): real headings stay.
- **Links.** Keep every internal and external link and its target. When you reword a sentence that holds a link, carry the link onto the equivalent, still-relevant anchor text; never drop it. Keep anchors descriptive, not "click here."
- **Images and alt text.** Leave image embeds and their alt text in place. Alt text is accessibility and SEO, so don't rewrite it into decoration or delete it.
- **Snippet and FAQ structure.** Keep the elements that win rich results: an answer-first sentence near the top of a section, numbered how-to steps as an actual numbered list, and FAQ items phrased as questions. Making a lede more "human" must not bury the direct answer three sentences down.
- **Meta and frontmatter.** Don't touch the meta title, meta description, URL slug, canonical tag, or frontmatter fields unless asked. (File mode, in `SKILL.md`, already leaves frontmatter and link targets untouched.)
- **CTA.** Keep the call to action and its link (see §25 in `references/patterns.md`). Replace a vague send-off with a specific CTA rather than deleting the close.
- **Topical depth.** "Compress the dull parts" (Your Task step 2 in `SKILL.md`) is not license to gut on-topic sections that exist for coverage. Cut genuine fluff; keep the substance a ranking page needs.

Before returning a marketing rewrite, confirm the primary keyword, every heading, every link, every image, and the CTA all survived.
